Glossary

TermMeaning
KernelSmall weight matrix sliding over input to compute features.
Feature mapActivation map output by a convolution.
PaddingValues added around input borders to control output size.
StrideStep size of the kernel; larger stride downsamples.
DilationSpacing within kernel taps to increase receptive field.

References

  • Krizhevsky et al. – ImageNet Classification with Deep Convolutional Neural Networks (AlexNet).
  • Simonyan & Zisserman – Very Deep Convolutional Networks (VGG).
  • He et al. – Deep Residual Learning for Image Recognition (ResNet).
  • Huang et al. – Densely Connected Convolutional Networks (DenseNet).
  • Szegedy et al. – Going Deeper with Convolutions (Inception).
  • Howard et al. – MobileNets; Tan & Le – EfficientNet.
