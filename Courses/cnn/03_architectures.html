3. Architectures

Historical evolution and modern design patterns.

On this page

Classic CNNs

  • LeNet-5: early conv-pool stacks; tanh activations.
  • AlexNet: ReLU, dropout, data augmentation; breakthrough on ImageNet.
  • VGG: deep stacks of 3×3 conv; simplicity at the cost of parameters.

Residual & Dense

ResNet uses identity shortcuts to enable very deep networks. DenseNet concatenates features from all previous layers within a block, improving feature reuse and efficiency.

Inception

Parallel branches with different kernel sizes plus 1×1 bottlenecks to reduce compute while capturing multi-scale features.

Mobile/Efficient

MobileNet uses depthwise separable convolutions; EfficientNet scales depth/width/resolution with a compound coefficient.
