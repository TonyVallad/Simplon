4. Training Recipes

Battle-tested defaults to train CNNs effectively.

Augmentations

  • RandomCrop/Resize, Flip, ColorJitter for classification.
  • RandAugment/AutoAugment; MixUp/CutMix for regularization.
  • Normalization: mean/std per dataset (e.g., ImageNet).
import torchvision.transforms as T
train_tfms = T.Compose([
  T.RandomResizedCrop(224), T.RandomHorizontalFlip(),
  T.ColorJitter(0.2,0.2,0.2,0.1),
  T.ToTensor(), T.Normalize(mean, std)
])

Optimizers & Schedules

AdamW or SGD+momentum with cosine decay and warmup are strong defaults.

optimizer = torch.optim.SGD(model.parameters(), lr=0.1, momentum=0.9, weight_decay=1e-4)
scheduler = torch.optim.lr_scheduler.CosineAnnealingLR(optimizer, T_max=epochs)

Regularization

  • Weight decay; label smoothing (e.g., 0.1) for classification.
  • Stochastic depth / DropPath in deep residual nets.

Mixed Precision

Use AMP to speed training and reduce memory; keep numerically sensitive ops in FP32 if needed.

Checkpoints

Save best-by-metric and latest; include optimizer/scheduler state, epoch, seeds, and config.
