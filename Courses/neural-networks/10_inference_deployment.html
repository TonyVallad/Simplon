10. Inference & Deployment

From trained weights to reliable production systems.

On this page

  • Export
  • Optimization
  • Scaling
  • Monitoring
  • Safety

Export

Use ONNX or TorchScript for portability. Bundle model version, preprocessing, and expected input shapes.

Optimization

  • Quantization: INT8/FP16 for latency and memory.
  • Pruning: remove redundant weights/channels.
  • Distillation: train a smaller student from a large teacher.

Scaling

Autoscale serving; pick batch size for throughput vs latency. Cache frequent requests. Use A/B or shadow deployments for safe rollouts.

Monitoring

  • Track latency, error rates, and resource usage.
  • Detect data and concept drift; retrain as needed.
  • Set up alerting on KPI degradations.

Safety & Reliability

  • Validate and sanitize inputs; enforce size/range limits.
  • Add canaries and circuit breakers; timeouts and retries.
  • Handle PII securely; audit and version models.
