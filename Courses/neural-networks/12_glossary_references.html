Glossary

TermMeaning
ActivationNon-linear function applied to layer outputs (ReLU, GELU, etc.).
BackpropagationGradient computation via the chain rule in reverse.
Batch sizeSamples per optimizer step.
EpochFull pass over the training set.
GeneralizationPerformance on unseen data.
OverfittingExcellent training error but poor validation/test error.
RegularizationTechniques to reduce overfitting (dropout, weight decay).
