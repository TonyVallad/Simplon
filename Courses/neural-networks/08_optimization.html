8. Optimization

Find good minima efficiently with the right optimizer and learning rate schedule.

SGD & Momentum

Strong generalization; momentum accelerates along low-curvature directions and damps oscillations. Nesterov lookahead can further stabilize.

Adam / AdamW

Adaptive per-parameter learning rates; fast convergence. Prefer AdamW (decoupled weight decay). Tune β₁/β₂ carefully for very long runs.

RMSProp

Normalizes updates by running average of squared gradients; simpler cousin to Adam.

Learning Rate Schedules

  • Cosine decay with warmup: robust default.
  • One-cycle: increases then decays; can improve speed and generalization.
  • ReduceLROnPlateau: adapt LR when validation stagnates.

Practical Tricks

  • Run an LR range test to find a good initial LR.
  • Use gradient clipping to prevent exploding gradients.
  • Zero gradients with set_to_none=True to save memory.
