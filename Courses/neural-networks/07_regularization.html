7. Regularization & Generalization

Prevent overfitting and improve robustness.

On this page

  • Early Stopping
  • Weight Decay
  • Dropout
  • Data Augmentation
  • Ensembling
  • Cross-Validation

Early Stopping

Monitor validation loss/metric and stop when it stops improving for N epochs (patience). Keep the best checkpoint.

Weight Decay

Penalize large weights (L2). Use AdamW to decouple weight decay from adaptive moments.

Dropout

Randomly zero units during training. Tune rate (e.g., 0.1–0.5). Consider SpatialDropout for CNNs.

Data Augmentation

  • Images: flip, crop, color jitter, MixUp, CutMix.
  • Text: dropout, synonym replacement, back-translation (task-dependent).
  • Time series: jitter, windowing, time-warping; preserve temporal semantics.

Ensembling

Average predictions from diverse models or checkpoints to improve stability and accuracy.

Cross-Validation

Use k-fold/group k-fold when data is scarce or split-induced variance is high. Report mean ± std across folds.
